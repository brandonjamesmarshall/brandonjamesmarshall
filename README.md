# Hey, I'm Brandon

Building **[Vibewatch](https://github.com/Vibewatch-io)** — community intelligence for web3 teams. It sits inside the servers and channels where your community actually talks (Discord, Telegram, X, and more) and turns the vibe into a weekly report your whole team can act on.

Before this I spent 5+ years running social and community for Bitcoin companies — Stacks Labs, Trust Machines, BlockFi. I was the one reading everything the community said, every day, and almost none of it reached the rest of the team. Vibewatch is me fixing that.
